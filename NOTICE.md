# Acceptable Use Notice

Autody supports two distinct uses: first-party or explicitly authorized creator analytics, and public topic research through `social-radar`. The analysis skills process owned/authorized Douyin and Xiaohongshu content and creator-center metrics. The radar reads normally accessible public works, comments and original-source references to prepare evidence-backed topic cards and user-selected script drafts.

Do not use Autody to access or infer private or non-public information from accounts, dashboards or browser sessions that you do not own or have permission to operate. Public research does not grant access to private creator analytics or permission to bypass verification. Preserve source attribution and keep captured material local; the package does not redistribute research evidence or publish generated drafts. Any misuse is the responsibility of the user.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable law, platform terms, privacy obligations, and data retention rules.
